OUR SERVICES

Healthcare Designed
Around You.

We provide comprehensive healthcare services for individuals, families and businesses. From routine consultations to specialised medical care, our clinic is committed to delivering compassionate, professional healthcare tailored to your needs.

Acute & Chronic Conditions

Comprehensive diagnosis, treatment and long-term management of acute illnesses and chronic medical conditions for patients of all ages.

Women's Health

Family planning, reproductive healthcare, cervical screening and pregnancy care.

Obstetric Ultrasound

Pregnancy ultrasound examinations to monitor your baby's health and development.

Men's Health

Routine check-ups, prostate health, sexual health and preventative care designed specifically for men.

HIV Management

Confidential counselling, HIV testing, treatment, monitoring and ongoing support.

Minor Surgeries

Safe outpatient procedures including wound care, abscess drainage, cyst removal and more.

Additional Medical Services

Our practice also offers a range of specialised healthcare services to support the health and wellbeing of every patient.

Blood Tests

Comprehensive laboratory testing for routine health screenings, diagnosis, disease monitoring and preventive healthcare.

DNA Testing

Confidential DNA testing services including paternity testing and medically approved family relationship assessments.

IV Drip Therapy

Intravenous hydration and vitamin therapy designed to improve wellness, energy and recovery.

Medical Reports

Professional medical reports, fitness assessments and documentation for work, school, insurance and legal purposes.

Disability Grants

Medical assessments and supporting documentation required for disability grant applications and medical reviews.

Specialist Referrals

Coordinated referrals to trusted specialists, hospitals and healthcare facilities whenever advanced care is required.
